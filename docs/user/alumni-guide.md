# AKPD Member Portal — Alumni Guide

Welcome back. The AKPD member portal lets you stay connected with the chapter, browse the alumni network, explore graduating seniors' profiles, and share opportunities with current members.

---

## Getting Started

1. Go to [PORTAL_URL] and click **Sign in with Google**.
2. Use the Google account associated with your registered email.
3. On your first login, you'll be prompted to set up your profile: enter your full name, your current city, and select your interests. Hit **Save** to finish.

---

## Your Profile & Settings

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner and select **Settings**.
2. Update any of the following: full name, LinkedIn URL, location, interests.
3. To change your profile photo, click **Upload Photo** and select an image.
4. Click **Save Changes** when done.

> Your location is used to place your pin on the People map so current members and other alumni can find you.

---

## People Map

The People map shows the full alumni and member network as pins on an interactive map.

1. Click **People** in the navigation bar.
2. Use the **search bar** on the left to find someone by name.
3. Use the **industry** or **interest** filters to narrow results.
4. Click any **pin** on the map to see a profile card: name, company, role, and contact info.

---

## Seniors Directory

Browse graduating seniors' career profiles to see where the next generation is headed.

1. Click **Seniors** in the navigation bar.
2. Use the **search bar** to find by name or major.
3. Use the **year** or **track** filter pills to narrow by graduation year or career track.
4. Click any card to open the full senior profile.

---

## Opportunities Board

Share job opportunities, internships, or openings at your company with current brothers.

**Browsing:**
1. Click **Opportunities** in the navigation bar.
2. Use the **type filters** (Internship, Full-Time, Club, Research) to browse listings.

**Posting:**
1. Click **Post Opportunity**.
2. Fill in the title, company, type, description, location, deadline, and link.
3. Click **Submit**.

> Posts require admin approval before appearing on the board — usually within 24 hours.

---

## FAQ

**Why can't I see Recruitment or Rate My Class?**
Those pages are available to active chapter members only. As an alumnus, you have access to the People map, Seniors directory, and Opportunities board.

**How do I update my location on the map?**
Go to **Settings** → update your **Location** field → **Save Changes**. Your pin updates automatically.
