# AKPD Member Portal — Alumni Quick Reference

## Sign In
Go to [PORTAL_URL] → **Sign in with Google** → complete the one-time profile setup (name, city, interests).

---

## Update Your Profile
Avatar (top-right) → **Settings** → update name, location, LinkedIn, interests, photo → **Save Changes**.
Your location appears as a pin on the alumni map.

---

## Browse the Alumni Map
**People** → search by name or filter by industry/interest → click a pin to view someone's profile.

---

## Post an Opportunity
**Opportunities** → **Post Opportunity** → fill in details → **Submit**.
Admin approves before it goes live — usually within 24 hours.

---
