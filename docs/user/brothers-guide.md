# AKPD Member Portal — Brothers Guide

Welcome to the Alpha Kappa Psi Delta member portal. This guide walks you through everything available to you as a brother.

---

## Getting Started

1. Go to [PORTAL_URL] and click **Sign in with Google**.
2. Use your Google account (UNC email or personal — whichever you registered with).
3. On your first login, you'll be prompted to set up your profile: enter your full name, your current city, and select your interests. Hit **Save** to finish.

---

## Your Profile & Settings

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner and select **Settings**.
2. Update any of the following: full name, LinkedIn URL, location, interests.
3. To change your profile photo, click **Upload Photo** and select an image (JPG or PNG).
4. Click **Save Changes** when done.

> Your location is used to place your pin on the People map.

---

## People Map

The People map shows alumni and members as pins on an interactive map.

1. Click **People** in the navigation bar.
2. Use the **search bar** on the left to find someone by name.
3. Use the **industry** or **interest** filters to narrow results.
4. Click any **pin** on the map to see a profile card: name, company, role, and contact info.

---

## Seniors Directory

Browse graduating seniors' career profiles.

1. Click **Seniors** in the navigation bar.
2. Use the **search bar** to find by name or major.
3. Use the **year** or **track** filter pills to narrow by graduation year or career track.
4. Click any card to open the full senior profile.

---

## Opportunities Board

Browse job and club postings, or share one of your own.

**Browsing:**
1. Click **Opportunities** in the navigation bar.
2. Use the **type filters** (Internship, Full-Time, Club, Research) to narrow listings.
3. Click any listing to view the full details and application link.

**Posting:**
1. Click **Post Opportunity**.
2. Fill in the title, company, type, description, location, deadline, and link.
3. Click **Submit**.

> Your post requires admin approval before it appears on the board. This typically takes less than 24 hours. Contact [ADMIN_EMAIL] if it's been longer.

---

## Recruitment Resources

Access career resources organized by track (Finance, Consulting, Tech, etc.).

1. Click **Recruitment** in the navigation bar.
2. Click a **career field** to expand its resources.
3. Click any **file** to download it, or any **link** to open it in a new tab.

> Files use secure, time-limited download links — if a download doesn't start, click again.

---

## Rate My Class

### Reviews Tab

Share your experience with a class to help brothers plan their schedules.

1. Click **Classes** in the navigation bar — you'll land on the **Reviews** tab.
2. Use the **search bar** to find reviews by course code or name.
3. Filter by **department**, **difficulty** (Easy / Moderate / Hard), **workload** (Light / Medium / Heavy), or toggle **Recommended Only**.
4. To write a review: click **Write a Review**, enter the course code and name, fill in your ratings, select any UNC focus areas, choose your semester and grade (optional), write your review, and click **Submit**.

> You can delete your own reviews at any time by clicking the delete button on your review card.

### Resources Tab

Upload or download study materials organized by course.

1. Click the **Resources** tab at the top of the Classes page.
2. Find your course's folder and click to expand it.
3. Click any **file** to download it, or any **link** to open it.
4. To upload: click **Add Resource** (or the **"Add to [COURSE]"** button inside a folder), choose **File Upload** or **Link/URL**, fill in the details, and click **Submit**.

> File uploads are limited to 50 MB. Supported formats: PDF, DOCX, PPTX, images, and more.

## FAQ

**Why isn't my opportunity post showing up?**
Posts require admin approval before appearing on the board. It usually takes less than 24 hours. If it's been longer, contact [ADMIN_EMAIL].

**How do I update my location on the map?**
Go to **Settings** → update your **Location** field → **Save Changes**. Your pin updates automatically.

**Can I delete a class review I posted?**
Yes. Find your review on the Classes → Reviews tab and click the delete icon on your own review.

**Why does my file download not start?**
Download links are time-limited for security. Click the download button again to generate a fresh link.
